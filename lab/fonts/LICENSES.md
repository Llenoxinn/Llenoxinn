Active profile fonts are released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The exact license text accompanies each family.

| Files | Family | Usage / source |
|---|---|---|
| `Inter-Bold.otf`, `Inter-SemiBold.otf`, `Inter-Regular.otf` | Inter | Headlines, labels and text, as on notion.com/product/dev; [rsms/inter v3.19](https://github.com/rsms/inter). License: `OFL-Inter.txt` |

| `PixelifySans-Bold.ttf` | Pixelify Sans | Pixel accents; copied from the portfolio. License: `OFL-PixelifySans.txt` |

The older font files are kept for reproducing earlier artwork. Their family licenses remain in this folder: Boldonse, Tektur, Jura, Geist Mono, Nothing You Could Do, and Big Shoulders.

Icons in `lab/icons.py` come from Simple Icons 13.21.0 (CC0 1.0), Devicon 2.17.0 (MIT) and Phosphor Icons 2.1.1 (MIT).
